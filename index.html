The Problem03

Your team. Your app. On the App Store.

A director fills out a form. Forty-eight hours later, their club's branded app is live on the App Store and Google Play.

  • Rosters via CSV upload or screenshot import; coaches, players, parents and organizers each with their own permissions.

  • Live streaming — every game, home or away, in the club's own app.
The Solution04

And an AI agent runs the club's front desk.

AppSpace doesn't just give directors software. It gives them staff.

  • Answers parents instantly — registration links, Zelle instructions, sign-in help, 24/7, in the club's voice.
  • Runs onboarding — imports rosters, links parents to kids, walks each family through registration and documents.
  • Manages the money trail — records offline Zelle payments, chases balances, marks paid on the director's confirmation.
  • Escalates only what needs a human — the director sees one clear item with the action, not a support inbox.

This desk is live today, running two clubs' customer operations end to end. A competitor has to hire staff to match it.
