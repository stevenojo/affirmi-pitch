The Problem02

One platform. Everything a club needs to collect.

  • Registration & payments — cards with fees shown up front, offline (Zelle/Venmo/cash) recorded in one tap, payment plans, discounts.
  • Documents — waivers and medical forms signed digitally; payment is blocked until every required signature is in.
  • Rosters & families — invite links, parents enter their own data, multiple kids on one account.
  • Team chat & notifications — routed to the right parents, not a group-chat soup.
  • Live streaming — every game, home or away, with live viewer counts. Branded club apps on iOS and Android.
  • AI agent desk — an agent works alongside each club: answers parents instantly, runs onboarding, records offline payments, and flags the director only when a human is actually needed. It already manages two clubs' customer conversations today.
  • Player development — stats, film review, highlight reels, and skill tracking that give families a visible return on their investment.

Market07

Model: we get paid when the club gets paid

  • Lakeshow was the learning deal — 3.2% platform fee, signed just to prove the rails on a real club. Card processing runs on the club's own Stripe account at Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢, so every dollar they collect is real production money.
  • Fury is the standard rate — signed at 4%, no learning deal, from the founder's own network, collecting within 3 days of going live.
  • The upgrade is already priced in — a club like Lakeshow at 4% instead of 3.2% is a 25% revenue lift on the same volume, with zero product change.
  • Offline payments count too — Zelle, cash and check are recorded in the app and billed, so the platform earns even when the card never runs.
